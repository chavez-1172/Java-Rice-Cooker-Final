What is a Loop?

A loop is a smart control structure that repeats a block of code over and over until a specific condition tells it to stop. It saves you time, keeps your code clean, and does all the repetitive work automatically!
For instance, a capybara wants to take a bath.



Capybara takes a bath once.
Capybara takes a bath twice.
Capybara takes a bath thrice.

This gets tiring as the process gets longer. What if capybara wants to take a bath for 1000 times!?
